# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of KickOnTheGo, based on OpenKick 1.4.

- Stream tabs: up to 5 channels open, only the active one plays.
- Following list in the player: loads when the player opens and refreshes every 2 minutes.
- Auto-reconnect when your phone switches between LTE and 5G (or Wi-Fi and cellular), and through dead zones.
- Audio only when the screen locks; tapping the notification opens the player with chat.
- Jumps back to live after a stall, never after you rewind on purpose.
- Kick green tab highlight.
- New name, icon and app id (`dev.sydnorlabs.kickonthego`), so it installs next to OpenKick.
