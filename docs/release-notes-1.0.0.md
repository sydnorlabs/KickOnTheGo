First release of KickOnTheGo, an unofficial Kick app for Android built on OpenKick 1.4 and made for listening on the go.

## What's new
- Stream tabs: keep up to 5 channels open, only the active one plays
- Following list in the player: loads when the player opens and refreshes every 2 minutes
- Auto-reconnect when your phone switches between LTE and 5G (or Wi-Fi and cellular), and through dead zones
- Audio only when your screen locks; tapping the notification opens the player with chat
- Jumps back to live after a stall, never after you rewind on purpose
- Kick green tab highlight

Everything else comes from OpenKick 1.4: Kick sign-in, live rewind, clips and VODs, downloads, 7TV emotes and chat replay.

## Install
Download `KickOnTheGo-1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your phone (Android 8.0 or later). It installs as its own app (`dev.sydnorlabs.kickonthego`), so it can sit next to OpenKick. For automatic updates, add this repo in Obtainium.
